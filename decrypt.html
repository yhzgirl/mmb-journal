Open an encrypted journal

This page turns the encrypted journal in your Dropbox back into plain files: a normal MMB Journal backup ZIP with readable entries, photos and videos. It works offline, and nothing leaves this device.

  1. In Dropbox, open Apps, then download the MMB Journal folder (Dropbox gives you a ZIP).
  2. Choose that ZIP below and type your recovery key.
  3. Save the plain backup it makes. It isn't encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe.
